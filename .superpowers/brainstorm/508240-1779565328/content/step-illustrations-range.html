Step 1 of 5
~ 3 min
Dice the onion finely
Halve through the root, peel, make horizontal cuts then slice down. Keep the root intact so it holds together as you work.
Step 2 of 5
~ 8 min
Soften the onion in oil
Cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until translucent and just starting to turn golden at the edges.
✦ Don't rush this — soft sweet onions are the base of the whole dish.
Step 3 of 5
~ 1 min
Bloom the spices
Add cumin, paprika and coriander directly to the pan. Stir constantly for 60 seconds until fragrant — the heat unlocks the oils.
✦ Pull from the heat if anything smells like it's burning. Scorched spices are bitter.
Step 4 of 5
~ 15 min
Simmer with the lid on
Reduce heat to low, cover, and let it bubble gently. Stir every few minutes and add a splash of water if the sauce thickens too fast.
Step 5 of 5
~ 2 min
Plate and serve immediately
Spoon the shakshuka straight from the pan. Top with fresh parsley and a drizzle of good olive oil. Serve with crusty bread alongside.
✦ Serve in the pan at the table for the best presentation and to keep it hot.
Step 3 of 6
~ 8 min
Knead until smooth
Push the heel of your hand into the dough, fold back, rotate 90°, repeat. You're done when the surface is smooth and springs back when poked.
✦ If the dough tears instead of stretching, rest it for 5 minutes and try again.
