Step 4 of 6
Sourdough
✕
~ 10 min
Knead and develop the gluten
Push the heel of your hand into the dough, fold back, rotate 90°, repeat. You're done when the surface is smooth, no longer sticky, and springs back when poked lightly with a finger. Don't skimp — underdeveloped gluten means a dense loaf.
✦ If the dough tears instead of stretching, rest it for 5 minutes and try again.
