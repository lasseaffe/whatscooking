What changed: Pinboard takes the full page width and is the first thing you see. Empty slots have dashed borders that signal "drop something here." CTA is "Build this week →" — clear and confident. Day labels are #EFE3CE — readable at a glance.
1
Pinboard prominence: moved from narrow sidebar to full-width hero above the calendar. The planning board IS the page — it should look like it.
2
CTA rename: "Weave the week →" → "Build this week →". Keeps forward momentum, passes the non-power-user test.
3
Empty slots: dashed-border cards with a "+" icon instead of invisible placeholder text. Users immediately understand these are droppable.
4
Day label contrast: column headers shift from #3a3a38 (invisible on dark) to #EFE3CE warm linen. The week overview becomes scannable.
