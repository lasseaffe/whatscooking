Section map — what scrolls vs. what slides

Each section uses the pattern that best fits its content. Scroll down through sections; inside some sections, content advances horizontally.

SCROLL ↓ ① Hero — "What are you cooking today?"

Full-height cinematic opener. Big typography, ambient recipe image or subtle grain texture behind it. One CTA: "Start cooking". Sets the mood. Vertical scroll reveals next section.

SLIDES → ② Feature showcase — 4 guided slides

Full-height section that locks scroll while you advance through 4 feature slides with left/right arrows. Each slide = one big idea with a mini mockup visual:

After slide 4, scroll unlocks and continues down. Dot indicators show progress.
