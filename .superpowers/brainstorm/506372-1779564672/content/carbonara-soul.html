What is the soul of this dish?
A
Restraint as mastery
Four ingredients. No shortcuts. The discipline of not adding cream when your instinct screams to. The design mirrors that — almost nothing on the page, and it says everything.
B
The moment of heat
That 30-second window where the egg either silks or scrambles. Tension, timing, irreversibility. The design captures that critical instant — hot pan energy frozen in print.
C
Ancient Rome in a bowl
Pork, egg, aged cheese — the same ingredients Roman soldiers ate. Continuity across two thousand years. The design draws on that weight — stone, inscription, monument.
D
The dish that starts arguments
No cream. Ever. It's the one Italian food rule that makes people angry. The poster leans into that tension — confrontational typography, something to fight about.
