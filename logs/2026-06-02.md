Reframed the WC feature from a collection-driven passport challenge to an allegiance-driven matchday experience. Shipped the launch-critical core loop: pick teams → see your matchdays → one-tap matchday menu. Passport demoted to a collapsed secondary section.

- **DB migration** `20260601120000_wc_team_following.sql`: `wc_user_teams` (followed nations, one primary) + `wc_watch_parties` (fixture↔event↔squad link) with RLS following the kitchen-groups conventions (reuses `is_group_member`). **Not yet applied to remote** — relies on the `supabase db push` CI pipeline on master.
