## [Wave 2] Watch squads + auto-suggested per-match events

Built the social layer: one tap on a matchday creates a real event with the matchday menu pre-seeded, optionally tied to a persistent watch squad.

### What was done
- **API** `POST /api/world-cup/watch-parties`: resolves a fixture → creates a `dinner_party` (titled "🇧🇷 Brazil vs Australia · Watch Party", scheduled at kickoff, WC theme) → seeds the matchday menu into `dinner_party_items` (snacks=`snack`, nation dishes=`meal`, with `recipe_id` when matched) → invites squad members as `editor` guests when a `kitchen_group_id` is passed → records the `wc_watch_parties` link (wrapped in try/catch so it's **non-fatal before the migration applies** — the event still gets created). Reuses existing `dinner_parties` / `dinner_party_items` / `kitchen_group_members` tables.
- **Component** `wc-watch-party-button.tsx`: a sheet that lazy-loads the user's squads (`GET /api/kitchen/groups`), lets them pick "Just me", an existing squad, or create a **new watch squad** inline (`POST /api/kitchen/groups`), then creates the watch party and routes to `/dinner-parties/[id]`. Auth-guarded (→ /login).
- **Auto-suggest** banner in `wc-my-matchdays`: for the soonest followed match, a "Rally your squad — [Team] play [Opp] tomorrow" prompt with a one-tap watch-party CTA. Replaced the old prefill-link CTA in each matchday row with the new sheet flow.
