"What's Cooking" Master Implementation Plan

This document serves as the comprehensive "North Star" for the development of the "What's Cooking" platform. It integrates visual identity, spatial logic, feature requirements, and technical execution strategies into a single source of truth.

1. Global Design System & Visual IdentityThe platform utilizes a "Premium Elevation" system to move away from a flat dark mode into a high-end, accessible digital product with depth.Color Theory & Depth (Lightened Dark Mode)To fix the "muddy" look of the previous version, use these hex codes strictly. Higher elevation is represented by lighter values.LevelRoleHex CodeNoteLevel 0Base Background#1F1B19Replaces #120F0D (Warm Charcoal)Level 1Surface (Cards/Sidebar)#2C2724Replaces #1E1A17 (Deep Cocoa)Level 2Elevation (Active/Tips)#3A3430Replaces #2A2521 (Muted Umber)SearchPrimary Tool#3A3430Lightest surface element for focusAccentsPrimary Actions (CTAs)Saffron / PersimmonHigh-chroma for "You are here" signalsLight Mode: Use a camel/beige tone instead of off-white to reduce brightness while maintaining a premium, organic feel.Nutrient Tracker: Use warmer, earthy tones (Orange/Beige/Camel) to differentiate these cards from the standard recipe feed.Typography & SpacingScale: 1.250 Major Third typographic scale.Vertical Rhythm: Line-height minimum 1.5x for body text to avoid "text-walling."Grid: Standard 8px base for all spacing and margins.Nested Radii: Follow the formula $R_{outer} = R_{inner} + Padding$ for balanced corners.

2. Information Architecture & NavigationSidebar RestructuringGroup items by User Intent (Discovering vs. Managing vs. Configuring) to reduce clutter. The sidebar animation should move separately from the main content.Main Navigation (Action-Oriented): Recipes (The Hub) and Meal Plans.Planning & Hosting (Premium): Dinner Party (specialized version of a meal plan).Kitchen Management: My Pantry and Shopping List.Health: Nutrient Tracker (grouped with Profile/Health data).Personal Space (Bottom-Anchored): Profile & Settings (combined).Refinement Rules:Remove sub-text descriptions under sidebar items; use clear icons and labels.Subcategories should extend directly to the right of the parent category.Top Navigation & FiltersFilter Drawer: Move Dietary Filters from the top bar into a progressive questionnaire drawer on the Recipes page (Category -> Dietary -> Difficulty -> Seasonality).Scramble Together: This function should be moved into the Pantry page rather than the main header.

3. Discovery & Browse ExperienceRecipe Card DesignChange the card color to a darker camel-beige.[Image: Recipe Card showing an image of White Beans with a 30-min tag and text below]Image Space: Increase the image area so tags like "Vegan" or "Mediterranean" are fully visible.Hover Effect: Implement a subtle zoom, color change, and a brief description text reveal.Content: Show the "nourishing description" (body text) in an extra textbox below the recipe name.World's Cuisine & Atlas[Image: Larger country cards with characteristic thumbnails and continent backgrounds]Visuals: Make country cards larger with high-quality, culturally accurate thumbnails.Continent Backgrounds: Apply specific background assets for each continent (e.g., paths to assets/worlds_cuisines).Interactive Map: Use a stylized SVG map. Countries "unlocked" by cooking a recipe should glow in Saffron/Persimmon.

4. The Recipe Execution Flow (Phases II - V)Phase II: Ingredients & Preparation[Image: Ingredients checklist with a Metric/Imperial toggle and serving size selector]Folding Logic: The ingredients section should fold completely to the left when cooking starts, leaving only an icon/text to expand it.Serving Multiplier: A "Serving Size" selector that dynamically updates ingredient quantities (e.g., 8 servings to 16 doubles all values).Unit Toggle: A slider to switch between Metric and Imperial units.Checklist: Interactive "cross-offs." The section should auto-collapse once 100% of items are checked.Phase III: The Cook Section (Focus Flow)[Image: Focused instruction step with a highlight tint and an SOS Helper at the bottom]Focus Mode: Auto-scroll to center the active step. Apply a #3A3430 background tint and a subtle shadow to the active instruction.Fixed Navigation: A persistent "Next Step" button at the bottom center.SOS Helper: A context-aware helper at the bottom of each step (e.g., if the step says "add salt," the SOS suggests "what to do if I added too much salt").Phase IV: Table Setting & ServingOccasion Tabs: Provide specific visualizations for "Casual," "Intimate," and "Festive" table settings.Visual Guides: Instructions on how to fold napkins and plate the food (e.g., "Generous heap in the center").Phase V: Restore & CleanupStorage Stats: Display "Hero Stats" for food care (e.g., "1-3 Days" in large font).Cleanup Concierge: A toggle to switch between "Dishwasher Safe" and "Hand Wash Only" items.5.
